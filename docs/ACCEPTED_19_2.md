# Accepted desktop baseline

Release 0.1.19-local.20261023 contains the accepted 0.1.17-local.20261019.2 pose algorithms.
Current-image fixed 15-degree RTMW observations may update qualified elbows, wrists and foot details after same-side torso checks. Knees/ankles retain primary observations.
Three actual images may provide a separate display estimate after bidirectional optical-flow checks. Raw model evidence and eligibility remain available; display estimates do not establish contact.
Round heads and independently verified real empty-wall backgrounds remain supported.
The later occlusion hold, experimental identity heads, PoseWarper and other rejected candidates are excluded. Long occlusion and exact sole contact remain unresolved.
The prior development measurements are not independent human-annotated accuracy or a guarantee on new videos. User records are not rewritten.
